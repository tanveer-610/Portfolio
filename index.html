PayNext: payment gateway

Payment Systems Operator platform · Aug 2025 – Present

Current

One integration for merchants to accept cards, mobile wallets and net banking. It covers checkout, a card-data vault, order processing, bank integrations, notifications, settlement batches and signed webhooks, all running as independent Spring Boot services.

  • Card-data security

    A per-session ECDH key encrypts card details in the browser (HKDF + AES-GCM). Only the card vault can decrypt them.

  • Payment channels

    Visa, Mastercard and AMEX through acquirer banks, plus bKash and City Touch, all behind one processor.

  • Signed webhooks (IPN)

    One final, HMAC-signed notification per order. It supports credential rotation and is safe to retry.

  • Notification platform

    Email, SMS, FCM push and webhooks, with provider chains, ordered failover and TOTP-based OTPs.

  • Rates & settlement

    Versioned TDR/MDR rates with effective windows, BIN ranges, multi-store merchants and batch reconciliation.

  • Key management

    Merchant API credentials are managed in one central service, which every other service relies on for authentication and signing.
